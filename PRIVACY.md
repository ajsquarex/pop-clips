# Privacy policy: POP YouTube Poster

POP YouTube Poster is a personal tool used only by the owner of this repository to upload
their own gameplay videos to their own YouTube channel on a schedule.

- It requests access only to upload and manage videos on the owner's channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share data about any other person.
- Access tokens are kept as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets in a private repository.
- The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: open an issue in this repository.
